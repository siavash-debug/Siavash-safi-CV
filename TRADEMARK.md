# LOTA.design Trademark & Brand Policy

This policy explains how the LOTA brand identifiers may be used, and how
brand rights relate to software licensing and copyright. It is not legal
advice, and it does not grant or describe rights beyond what it states.

## Brand

LOTA, LOTA.design, the LOTA logo, the LOTA wordmark, and the associated
visual identity are brand identifiers of the LOTA.design project and
platform, associated with Siavash Safi. The primary project domain is
lota.design.

The use of the ™ symbol indicates an asserted trademark designation;
it does not represent a claim of registered trademark status. No statement
in this policy asserts that any LOTA mark is a registered trademark in any
jurisdiction.

These identifiers are brand assets. They are governed by this policy and
not by the software terms in LICENSE.

## Permitted uses

Without asking first, you may:

- refer to LOTA or LOTA.design by name to identify the project factually
  and accurately;
- link to the official project or its official repository or website;
- discuss, review, write about, or compare the project, including
  critical commentary, using its actual name;
- state factually that software or material is part of, or originates
  from, LOTA.design when that is accurate;
- nominative references to the brand in ordinary technical discussion.

Ordinary, truthful references to the name are not prohibited by this
policy.

## Prohibited and restricted uses

The following require prior written permission from Siavash Safi and are
not granted by this repository:

- using the LOTA logo, wordmark, or visual identity as the logo or brand
  of a different product or service;
- creating a name, logo, or identity confusingly similar to LOTA or
  LOTA.design for a related product or service;
- presenting an unrelated product, service, or repository as LOTA.design
  or as an official LOTA project;
- using LOTA branding in a way that implies endorsement, sponsorship, or
  affiliation that does not exist;
- rebranding a modified or derivative version as LOTA.design or as an
  official LOTA release;
- using the LOTA logo or wordmark as part of a competing product identity.

This policy applies to the project's branding and identifiers. It does not
claim to restrict every use of the word "LOTA" everywhere, and it does not
restrict truthful, non-misleading references to the project.
